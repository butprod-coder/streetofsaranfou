# Effets de feu

Outil imagegen intégré, PNG transparent conservé sans retouche. Grille 4 × 3 : mégots, flammes, détonation.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production transparent PNG pixel art spritesheet for a 1990s arcade beat em up. Create ONE cohesive FIRE EFFECTS animation atlas, exactly 4 columns by 3 rows on a landscape 1536x1152 canvas. Every cell equally sized, generously separated, objects stay within central 75% of their cell. Genuine transparent RGBA background, zero alpha in empty regions, NO checkerboard, NO backdrop, NO floor, NO labels, NO grid, no characters. Crisp hand-pixelled dark outlined clusters, arcade orange/gold flames, not photorealistic. ROW 1: four successive animation frames of a small burning discarded cigarette butt flying horizontally: recognizable tan filter, short white paper cylinder, charred gray ash tip with bright red ember, a few tiny sparks and wisps of smoke. Same size all four frames. ROW 2: four frames of a looping ground fire patch, wide low cluster of several orange flames with yellow-white core, dark ember base, side view with slightly visible elliptical ground footprint but NO painted floor; subtle varying flame tongues, same footprint and size each frame. ROW 3: four consecutive frames of a small fiery ground detonation: initial bright ignition, expanding orange flame burst, dissipating flame and sparks, fading smoke and embers. Clear contained silhouettes, transparent gaps through smoke. Each cell independently usable as a sprite, every effect completely inside its cell and with generous transparent margins. No red targeting circles or UI overlays. The fire patch should be visually readable when drawn 130 pixels wide in the game.
