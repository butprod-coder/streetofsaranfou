# Décors en chaîne et adversaires

Sprites créés et détourés avec l’outil intégré imagegen. Images finales avec alpha : `chain-scenery-v1.png` (3 × 3) et `dynamic-enemies-v1.png` (4 × 3), 1536 × 1024. Le moteur isole les silhouettes des adversaires pour enlever les fragments de cellules voisines.

## Décors — génération

Use case stylized-concept. Production sprite atlas for retro pixel art street beat em up. Exact 3 columns by 3 rows grid, nine isolated centered sprites, generous empty gutters. Transparent alpha background ONLY, no gradient or matte or backdrop, no text. Row1: red explosive fuel barrel with yellow warning diamond; blue gray damaged electrical street cabinet with yellow lightning symbol; red fire hydrant. Row2: cracked fuel barrel; sparking opened electrical cabinet; broken hydrant spraying water. Row3: isolated orange explosion; isolated cyan electric arc burst; isolated horizontal white blue water jet pointing right. Detailed crisp 16-bit pixel art dark outlines. All objects fully contained in equal cells, landscape1536x1024.

## Adversaires — génération

Use case stylized-concept. Production transparent sprite atlas for retro 16-bit pixel art side scrolling street beat em up. EXACT 4 columns by 3 rows, 12 equal cells with generous transparent gutters. Three fictional adult street rivals, each row one consistent identity. Everyone faces right in side three quarter view. Row1 bulky street guard navy clothing wearing helmet and carrying large riot shield in front: idle stance, walking pose, forward shield bash attack, fallen defeated on ground with shield aside. Row2 lean reckless thug orange jacket with unmistakable cartoon bomb strapped to vest: idle, running, arming bomb with blinking fuse, fallen defeated with extinguished bomb. Row3 masked street shooter gray purple hood clothing holding long rifle: idle, walking, aiming firing rifle right, fallen defeated rifle lowered. Full body including feet in every cell, no overlap. Dark crisp outlines bold arcade colors, no text no logos. TRUE transparent alpha background no backdrop no glow no gradient. Landscape1536x1024.
